# Watchers – Movie & TV Recommendation Platform

**Discover. Watch. Review. Connect.**

> 🚧 **Project Status: Under Development**

## About the Project

Watchers is an AI-powered community platform for movie and TV enthusiasts, inspired by Goodreads. The idea is to create a space where people can discover what to watch next, share opinions, write genuine reviews, rate content, and connect with others who have similar interests.

The platform aims to combine **personalized content discovery with social interaction**, making watching movies and TV shows a more engaging and community-driven experience.

## Core Idea

- **AI-Powered Recommendations:** A hybrid recommendation system combining content-based filtering, collaborative filtering, and RAG to automatically suggest the **Top 5 Next Watches** based on user preferences, viewing history, and community reviews.

- **Group Recommendations:** A recommendation engine that analyzes multiple users' preferences, measures taste compatibility, and suggests movies or TV shows everyone in a group might enjoy.

- **Community Engagement:** Users can join interest-based communities, participate in discussions, share experiences, and interact with fellow binge-watchers.

- **Reviews & Ratings:** A dedicated space for genuine audience reviews, critic opinions, ratings, and spoiler-controlled discussions.

- **Personalized Watchlists:** Users can organize their watch history, track upcoming watches, and create collaborative watchlists with friends.

## Vision

To build a **interactive community for binge-watchers**, where AI simplifies content discovery and shared interests bring people together.

The goal is not just to recommend what to watch, but to create a community around the entire watching experience.
